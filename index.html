02 — Why ARCO

Built for how Indian businesses actually work.

GST from the ground up

CGST, SGST and IGST invoicing with HSN codes, delivery challans, day books, ledgers and tax registers — no add-ons.

WhatsApp at the core

Purchase orders, delivery alerts, payment links and marketing go out on the channel your customers already use.

Keeps working offline

Billing and job cards carry on without internet and sync automatically when you are back online.

Multi-branch by design

Branch-wise stock, orders and staff with one live view for head office — and full records for every transfer.

Role-based access

Owners, sales, warehouse, lab, accounts and delivery each see only what they need.

Web, desktop and mobile

A fast desktop workstation for billing and the lab floor, plus mobile apps for field teams and delivery staff.
