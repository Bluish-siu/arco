01 — The 4 notification pillars

Automations that pay for themselves.

ARCO listens to your Shopify store in real time and sends the right WhatsApp message at the right moment.

PILLAR 01

Abandoned cart drip recovery

Tracks every checkout, formats the cart, adds a discount code and queues a recovery message with a one-click link back to the cart. If the customer completes the order, the scheduled drip is cancelled automatically.

PILLAR 02

Instant order confirmation

Branded WhatsApp receipts the moment an order is placed — order number, line items, total and shipping destination.

PILLAR 03

Cash-on-delivery anti-RTO

COD orders get an interactive "Reply 1 to confirm, 2 to cancel" prompt. ARCO tags the Shopify order COD-Confirmed or COD-Cancelled, cutting return-to-origin courier losses.

PILLAR 04

Shipping & live courier tracking

When an order ships, ARCO picks up the carrier — BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, Shiprocket and more — and sends the buyer a live tracking link.

04 — Direct on Meta

The official WhatsApp Cloud API. No broker in between.

ARCO connects straight to Meta's WhatsApp Business Cloud API instead of going through a third-party broker.

Lower latency

Messages go straight from ARCO to Meta, with two-way webhooks updating your inbox in real time.

No intermediary markup

You pay Meta's conversation pricing — not a reseller's margin on top of it.

Native message formats

Interactive buttons, copy-code OTPs, rich media headers and delivery/read receipts.

Delivered, even outside 24 hours

Order updates that always reach the phone.

WhatsApp only allows free-form messages within 24 hours of the customer's last message. ARCO sends order updates on an approved utility template, so they are delivered at any time and aren't limited by marketing frequency caps.

06 — Security & compliance

Enterprise-grade under the hood.

Secure sign-in

Google OAuth 2.0 and WhatsApp OTP codes that expire in 5 minutes with limited attempts.

Verified webhooks

Every Shopify and Meta webhook is HMAC-SHA256 verified with timing-safe comparison.

Encrypted store tokens

Shopify access tokens are stored AES-256 encrypted, with HTTPS enforced everywhere.

Zero message loss

Events are written to a durable log before they are acknowledged, and duplicates are ignored.

GDPR ready

Customer data-request and redact webhooks required by the Shopify App Store are in place.

Multi-tenant

Every merchant's contacts, conversations and automations are kept separate.
