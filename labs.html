01 — See it at work

Every lab job, under control.

One lab ERP for every branch and the head office, with live data. These are real screens from Arco Labs.

1Inventory dashboard

Every stock item in one view

An executive command centre for stock balances, procurement activity and warehouse load, live for each branch.

  • Total value, item count, available quantity and warehouses
  • Low-stock, out-of-stock, pending POs and reorders flagged
  • Quick actions: goods receipt, consumption and stock adjustment
  • Export the whole dashboard to PDF in one click
2Material requisitions

Purchasing with approvals

A central procurement desk: branches raise requirements, head office reviews them, assigns suppliers and places orders.

  • Branches raise material requirements to head office
  • The national inventory head assigns approved vendors
  • POs sent on WhatsApp; invoices confirmed before payment
  • Filter by status, priority, department and invoice state
3Rx order entry

Speed-punched Rx orders

Built for the order desk: move through every field with the keyboard, then forward the order straight to production.

  • Brand, Rx type, index, coating, tint, DIA and fitting type
  • SPH, CYL, axis, add, prism and corridor for each eye
  • Tab and Enter to move fast; discount and GST worked out
  • Save as draft, forward the order or open the order queue
